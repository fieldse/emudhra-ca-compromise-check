Certificate matcher

Every bundle, keystore and binary the check reads goes through one matcher. It finds the 16 compromised intermediates, the three G3 roots with their two cross-certificates and AAA Certificate Services by the SHA-256 of each PEM block, by their DER bytes and by the first base64 line, and by public key when no full certificate is there. A full intermediate or G3 root is FAIL, AAA is WARN, and a bare intermediate key is INFO, because fixed software ships those keys as a blocklist.
