Claims and their sources

  • Claims are in our words, one per row. A row gives the item, the claim, then the value it states and a note when it has them.
  • Sources link the vendor, project or tracker document behind the claim, with its publisher and date. A source is primary unless it says secondary. Its S-number is its row in docs/sources.md in the repository.
  • Checked is the date the claim was last checked against its sources.
  • Verified rows say so in the note when the claim was checked by inspecting the package, image, binary or file itself, in a container.
  • Disputed rows state both sides and link both sources.
  • Each row has its own anchor, for example references.html#r005.

R001

Eight eMudhra G3B and G3C intermediatesEight eMudhra intermediates made at a 30 April 2025 key ceremony have P-384 private keys below 256, so anyone can recover the keys from the public certificates. Six are TLS CAs under emSign Root TLS CA - G3 and two are code-signing CAs under emSign Root CS CA - G3. The G3B CAs belong to eMudhra BV (Netherlands) and the G3C CAs to eMudhra Inc (US). They came from a batch of 24 intermediates generated that day, and only the P-384 keys of series B and C are affected.

ValuePrivate keys: 4, 56, 66, 115, 155, 156, 230, 237
